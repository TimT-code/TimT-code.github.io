Authors: Tim Tesner & Gemini 3 Pro

V12.2 Upgrades & Fixes:
- Critical Boot Fix: Restored hidden HTML file inputs that caused the engine initialization to crash.
- Detachable Master Control Buttons: Click and drag buttons like [PANIC] or [MUTE] off the control bar to place them permanently on the playfield for quick access.
- Group Tabs: The bottom HUD now supports Tabs [1-5]. You can switch groups instantly. Change the HUD background color via the Visuals menu.
- Asset Manager & Directory Loading: Use the [ASSETS] window to upload entire folders of sounds. Click "Spawn Pad" to generate a pad from the list.
- Advanced Pad Mixing: Every custom pad now has its own EQ (Low/Mid/High), Reverb/Delay Sends, and advanced looping (Loop X times).
- Perspective Engine: Check "Perspective" in Grid Setup to make the entire playfield and background visually tilt and swell with your sensor movements.
- True P-Mode Presets: The Preset Manager is now its own window, expandable up to 20 slots, with "Warn Before Load" safety checks.
