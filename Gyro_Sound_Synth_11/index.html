Key Features (V11.1)

  • PC Mouse Fling Emulation: Left-click and rapidly swipe/fling your mouse to perfectly mimic device accelerometer swings for Impact Modes!
  • Mobile Touch Saving: Use the [P-MODE: LOAD/SAVE] toggle to save Master Configs to P1-P4 directly on touchscreens!
  • True Polyphonic Touch/Strumming: Modes 6 & 7 instantly snap to touch. Hold notes to sustain them. Drag across the screen to strum multiple pads (polyphony).
  • Emergency UI Dodge: Floating GUIs will instantly become transparent and shrink if they overlap the critical Stop/Mute/Panic buttons while your mouse/finger approaches them.
  • Global Silence Memory: [⏸ SILENCE] pauses all samplers precisely at their millisecond position.
  • Independent Audio Pad Ecosystem: Audio pads bypass the master mute, have individual controls, and accept server-side URL paths directly.
